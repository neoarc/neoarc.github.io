# About Stage Contests

Stage Contests are events voluntarily organized by members of the SFMB community. Each contest may have its own theme, entry requirements, schedule, judging method, and prizes. The organizer's announcement is the authoritative source for the rules of an individual contest.

This page records ongoing contests and preserves the results of past contests, including their winners and winning levels.
