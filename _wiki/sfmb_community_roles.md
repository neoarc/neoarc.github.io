# About Community Roles

Community roles recognize verified contributions, identify experienced members, or grant responsibilities within the SFMB community.

Special Roles earned through contributions are **never granted automatically**. After meeting the minimum requirement, a member must request the role from a Moderator. The Moderator reviews the relevant contributions and grants the role manually.

# Special Roles

A member must hold at least one of the Special Roles listed below to post in `#request`.

| Role | Minimum requirement |
|:---|:---|
| **Bug Finder** | Report at least 5 bugs. |
| **Sound Producer** | Contribute at least 5 sound resources. |
| **Spriter** | Contribute at least 5 sprite resources. |
| **Stage Creator** | Have at least 10 uploaded stages rated 4 or higher. |
| **Translator** | Contribute at least 1 text resource. |

## Bug Finder

Bug Finder recognizes members who have helped improve the game through bug reports. After reporting at least five bugs, ask a Moderator to review the reports and grant the role manually.

## Sound Producer

Sound Producer recognizes members who have contributed at least five sound resources through the community's sound-resource channel.

## Spriter

Spriter recognizes members who have contributed at least five sprite resources through the community's sprite-resource channel.

## Stage Creator

Stage Creator recognizes members with at least ten uploaded stages that each have a rating of 4 or higher.

## Translator

Translator recognizes members who have contributed at least one text resource, such as an in-game translation.

# Stage Moderator

Stage Moderators help keep Stage World ratings fair by playing and rating uploaded stages.

Members may apply for the role when all of the following are true:

* They spend a significant amount of time playing SFMB.
* They play most of the stages being uploaded.
* They have played more than 500 stages.
* They do not have the `Shitposter` role.

Meeting these conditions does not grant the role automatically. Applicants must be reviewed before receiving Stage Moderator permissions.

# Contest Roles

## Contest Manager

The Contest Manager role was created to organize Stage Contests. It is currently held by **Abacta**.

## Contest Staff

Contest Staff members evaluate contest levels and are selected by the Contest Manager. The current Contest Staff members are **@Cloudz**, **@Jorden**, **@skillaway**, and **@Marci599**.

# Staff Roles

## Moderator

Moderators manage the community, enforce its rules, maintain community channels, manually grant eligible roles, and report major service problems to the Developer. This is an appointed responsibility rather than a role earned through a fixed contribution count.

See [[sfmb_betatest_community_moderator]]{Moderator} for the current list of responsibilities.

## Developer

The Developer role is currently assigned only to **neoarc**, the project's developer. It cannot be obtained through community contributions or by requesting the role.

# Requesting a Role

To receive a Special Role, contact a Moderator after meeting its minimum requirement. Provide enough information to locate the relevant bug reports, resources, or stages. The Moderator verifies the contributions and assigns the role manually.
